# User Manual: Intelligent Data Analysis Assistant

## 1. Getting Started
Welcome! This guide will help you use the assistant to analyze your data.

### 1.1. Uploading Data
1. Open the application.
2. On the left sidebar, click the "Browse files" button.
3. Select a CSV or Excel file from your computer.
4. The application will automatically load the data and show you a preview.

## 2. Asking Questions
You can interact with your data using plain English.
- In the "Ask a Question About Your Data" text box, type your query.
- **Examples:**
  - "What is the total sales?"
  - "Show the relationship between age and income"
  - "Summarize the customer feedback column"

The AI will interpret your question, perform the correct analysis, and explain its reasoning.
